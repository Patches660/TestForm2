Eldoria Expedition Terms & Conditions

1. Eligibility & Age Restrictions

Applicants must be between 18 and 50 years of age at the time of application. All candidates must possess valid identification or passport and demonstrate physical fitness required for strenuous remote excavation.

2. Operational Safety & Field Conduct

Members agree to abide by all expedition safety protocols, environmental protection directives, and chain of command orders. High-altitude and wilderness navigation require strict adherence to team guidance.

3. Archaeological Heritage & Discoveries

All antiquities, manuscripts, geological specimens, and architectural findings remain the absolute property of the Expedition Heritage Trust. Unauthorized collection or concealment of relics is strictly prohibited.

4. Compensation & Salary Tiers

Weekly disbursements are calculated according to proven archaeological experience verified by credentials. The base expedition stipend ranges from $700 up to $2,250 per week based on designated skill levels.

5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Expedition coordinates, site maps, and archaeological records are confidential until official scholarly publication. Participants must not disclose sensitive coordinates to external parties.
